A premium dishwasher is engineered to clean thoroughly, quietly and efficiently. Most disappointing results do not come from a fault. They come from loading, maintenance and water conditions. Here is how to get the results the machine was designed for.

Load for the water, not for space

  • Scrape food into the bin. There is no need to pre-rinse. Modern detergents work better with some soil to act on.
  • Face the dirty side of plates and bowls toward the center, where the spray is strongest.
  • Place glasses and cups on the upper rack, angled so water drains off.
  • Keep large pans and cutting boards along the sides and back, where they will not block the spray arms or detergent dispenser.
  • Turn each spray arm by hand before starting. If it catches on anything, rearrange.
  • Wash wooden utensils, cast iron, fine crystal and hand-painted china by hand.

Clean the filter

Many premium dishwashers use a manual filter instead of a noisy grinder. That keeps them quiet, but the filter needs attention.

  1. Make sure the dishwasher is off and cool.
  2. Remove the lower rack, then twist and lift out the filter assembly.
  3. Rinse it under warm running water. Use a soft brush for stubborn residue.
  4. Check the sump area for glass fragments or labels, then refit the filter until it locks.

Once a month is a good routine. Clean it more often if you run the dishwasher daily.

Use rinse aid

Rinse aid is not optional on a premium machine. It helps water sheet off dishes, which prevents spots and is essential for effective drying, especially with plastics. Keep the reservoir filled. If glasses show streaks, lower the dosage setting. If they show droplets or spots, raise it.

Manage hard water

Much of Southern California has hard water. Minerals leave a white film on glassware and build up inside the machine over time.

  • If your dishwasher has a built-in water softener, set it to your local water hardness and keep the salt reservoir filled with dishwasher salt.
  • Your water utility can tell you the hardness level for your address.
  • Run a dishwasher cleaner through an empty cycle every one to three months.
  • Run the kitchen tap until the water is hot before starting a cycle.

To check whether film is mineral or etching, soak a glass in white vinegar for five minutes. If the film dissolves, it is mineral and can be managed. If it remains, the glass may be etched, which is permanent.

When to call a technician

  • Water remains in the tub after a cycle, even with a clean filter.
  • You see water on the floor or beneath the unit.
  • Dishes are still dirty after you have corrected loading and cleaned the filter.
  • The cycle stops, displays an error code or the door does not stay in position.

For any leak, turn off the dishwasher and avoid further cycles until it has been inspected. Turn off power at the breaker before reaching under the unit.
