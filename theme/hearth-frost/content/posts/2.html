A professional range is built for daily use. Its burners, grates and knobs are designed to handle heat and heavy pans. They still need care. A few simple habits keep ignition reliable, flames even and finishes looking new.

Before you start

Always clean a cold range. Make sure every burner is off and the grates are cool to the touch. If you plan to remove burner caps or heads, turn off power to the range at the circuit breaker so the igniters cannot spark. Never spray cleaner directly onto the control panel or into burner ports.

Burners

  1. Lift off the burner caps and wash them in warm, soapy water. Rinse and dry them completely.
  2. Wipe the burner bases with a damp cloth. Clear any blocked ports with a straightened paper clip or a fine brush. Do not use toothpicks, which can break off inside.
  3. Keep the igniters dry. A wet igniter will click continuously until it dries.
  4. Replace each cap so it sits flat and centered. A cap that is slightly off will produce an uneven or lifting flame.

A healthy flame is blue with a small, steady tip. Yellow, noisy or uneven flames after cleaning are worth a professional look.

Grates

  • Let grates cool fully before cleaning. Sudden temperature changes can damage the finish.
  • Soak heavily soiled grates in hot, soapy water, then scrub with a non-abrasive pad.
  • Check your owner's guide before using the dishwasher. Some porcelain-coated grates can be washed there. Others should be cleaned by hand.
  • Dry grates thoroughly before returning them, and set them back in their proper positions so pans sit level.

Knobs and control panel

  • Most knobs pull straight off. Wash them in warm, soapy water and dry them before replacing.
  • Wipe the control panel with a soft cloth dampened with mild soap and water. Avoid abrasive cleaners, which can wear away the markings.
  • Do not let water drip behind the knobs. Moisture there can affect the ignition switches.

Stainless steel

Clean stainless surfaces with a soft cloth, following the direction of the grain. A dedicated stainless steel cleaner will help with fingerprints. Never use steel wool or chlorine bleach.

When to call a technician

  • You smell gas. If the odor is strong, leave the home and call your gas utility from outside first.
  • A burner will not light, or clicks continuously after the area is dry.
  • Flames remain yellow, uneven or lift off the burner.
  • A knob feels loose, will not turn or does not return to off.
  • The lowest simmer setting goes out or runs too hot.
