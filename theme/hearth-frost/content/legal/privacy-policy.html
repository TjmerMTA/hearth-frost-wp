3. How we use information

We use personal information for the following business purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule, confirm, reschedule and perform service visits.
  • To diagnose appliances, prepare estimates, order the correct parts and complete repairs.
  • To process in-warranty claims with the manufacturer when you ask us to.

  • To send appointment reminders, arrival notifications and service follow-ups.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information, such as Social Security numbers, health information or precise geolocation. 4. How we share information

We share personal information only as described below, and only to the extent needed.

  • Our technicians and staff, who need your details to plan and perform your visit.
  • Parts suppliers and manufacturers, to order parts for your appliance and to submit and process warranty claims. A manufacturer may apply its own privacy policy to information it receives for a warranty claim.
  • Payment processors, who handle card and bank payments under their own security standards.

  • Legal and safety: when required by law, subpoena or court order, or when we believe disclosure is needed to protect the rights, property or safety of any person.
  • Business transfers: if our business is sold, merged or reorganized, customer records may transfer to the successor, subject to this policy.
  • With your direction: for example, sharing a service report with your home warranty company or property manager when you ask us to.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

5. Cookies and analytics

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for it to function, such as remembering form progress and protecting against spam. With your consent where required, we also use analytics cookies to measure traffic and improve the Site.

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Use the cookie settings on the Site, where available, to accept or decline non-essential cookies.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Essential features may not work without them.
  • Use opt-out tools offered by analytics providers.

Global Privacy Control. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for that browser and any profile we associate with it. When we detect a GPC signal, we also turn off non-essential cookies for that browser. Our Site does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" signals, because there is no common standard for them.

  • Website analytics data: up to 26 months.

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or de-identify it. We may keep information longer where the law requires it or to resolve disputes.

10. Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, the CCPA gives you the following rights, subject to certain exceptions.

Your rights

  • Right to know. You may ask what personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, the purposes for collecting it, the categories of third parties we disclose it to, and the specific pieces of personal information we hold.
  • Right to delete. You may ask us to delete personal information we collected from you. We may keep information we need for legal, warranty, security or transaction purposes permitted by law.
  • Right to correct. You may ask us to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information. You may still submit an opt-out request, and we honor GPC signals as described in Section 5.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not ask for sensitive personal information. Any we receive is used only for purposes permitted by the CCPA, such as providing the Services you request, and never to infer characteristics about you. You may still ask us to limit its use.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will not deny service, charge a different price or provide a different quality of service because you exercised your privacy rights.

Categories of personal information

In the past 12 months we have collected the following categories, as described in Section 2: identifiers (name, email, phone, address, IP address); customer records (billing and service history); commercial information (services and parts purchased); internet or other electronic network activity (Site usage data); audio and visual information (call recordings and photos you send); and approximate location derived from IP address. We disclose these categories for business purposes to the recipients listed in Section 4.
