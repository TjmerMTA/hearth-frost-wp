A professional range should respond the moment you turn the knob. Even ignition, a steady simmer and a precise oven are the standard. When any of these slip, our technicians restore them, using genuine parts and a careful, methodical approach.

What we service

  • Gas ranges
  • Gas and induction rangetops
  • Induction cooktops
  • Gas and electric cooktops
  • Integrated griddles, charbroilers and French tops
  • Wall, island and insert ventilation hoods
  • Downdraft ventilation systems

Common problems we fix

  • Burners that click continuously or will not ignite
  • Uneven, yellow or lifting flames
  • Simmer settings that run too hot or go out
  • Oven not reaching or holding temperature
  • Induction zones that do not detect cookware or show error codes
  • Control knobs or touch controls that do not respond
  • Ventilation fans that are noisy, weak or will not start
  • Faint gas odor near the appliance

How a visit works

  1. You describe the symptom and share the model number. We confirm an arrival window.
  2. Your technician protects the surrounding floor and counters, then tests ignition, gas pressure, burners, oven temperature and controls.
  3. You receive a written estimate and approve it before any work begins.
  4. We repair, calibrate and test the appliance with you before we leave.

Questions

I smell gas. Should I book a visit?

If the smell is strong, leave the home, avoid using switches or flames, and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Once the utility has made the area safe, we can schedule a visit to find and repair the cause.

Why does my burner keep clicking after it lights?

The most common causes are moisture or food residue on the igniter, a misaligned burner cap or a worn spark module. Many cases are simple to correct. We diagnose the exact cause before recommending a repair.

Can you calibrate my oven temperature?

Yes. We measure the actual cavity temperature against the set point and calibrate the control. If a sensor or element is at fault, we will tell you.

Do you install new ranges?

Our focus is diagnosis, repair and maintenance. For new installations, we recommend a qualified installer or your appliance dealer.
