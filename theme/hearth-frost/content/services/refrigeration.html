A built-in refrigerator is designed to run quietly for decades. When it stops holding temperature, the food inside is not the only thing at stake. Our technicians work on Sub-Zero refrigeration every day. We find the cause, explain it plainly and repair it with genuine parts.

What we service

  • Built-in side-by-side and over-and-under refrigerators
  • Column refrigerators and freezers
  • Fully integrated, panel-ready units
  • Under-counter refrigerators, freezers and refrigerator drawers
  • Wine storage and beverage centers
  • Ice makers and water dispensers
  • Water filtration and air purification systems
  • Legacy models still in daily use

Common problems we fix

  • Refrigerator or freezer running warm
  • Frost or ice building up on the evaporator or back wall
  • Compressor running constantly, or not at all
  • Water pooling under crisper drawers or on the floor
  • Ice maker not producing, or producing small cubes
  • Doors that do not seal, or gaskets that are torn
  • Unusual noises, clicking or vibration
  • Error codes or an unresponsive control panel

How a visit works

  1. You request a visit and share the model and serial number. We confirm an arrival window.
  2. Your technician arrives with shoe covers and protective mats, then runs a full diagnosis of the sealed system, airflow and controls.
  3. You receive a clear written estimate. Nothing is repaired until you approve it.
  4. We complete the repair, often on the same visit, verify temperatures and leave the kitchen as we found it.

Questions

Is it worth repairing an older Sub-Zero refrigerator?

Usually, yes. These units are built for long service, and most failures involve components that can be replaced. During the diagnosis we give you an honest view of the unit's condition, so you can decide with full information.

What should I do while I wait for the technician?

Keep the doors closed as much as possible. Move perishable food to a cooler if temperatures rise above 40°F. Do not attempt to defrost the unit with heat guns or sharp tools.

Do you repair sealed systems?

Yes. Our technicians diagnose and repair sealed-system faults, including leaks, compressor issues and refrigerant recharges, where repair is practical.

Do you use genuine parts?

We install genuine OEM parts. If a part must be ordered, we tell you the expected timing before we schedule the return visit.
