The best repair is the one you never need. Regular maintenance keeps premium appliances efficient, quiet and precise. It also lets us find small issues before they become expensive ones.

What we service

  • Condenser cleaning for built-in, column and under-counter refrigeration
  • Door gasket inspection and replacement
  • Water and air filter changes
  • Burner inspection, cleaning and calibration
  • Oven temperature calibration
  • Steam oven descaling and inspection
  • Dishwasher filter, spray arm and drain service
  • Annual maintenance plans for the whole kitchen

Common problems we fix

  • Refrigeration running longer and louder than it should
  • Rising energy use from a dusty condenser
  • Gaskets that have hardened, split or lost their seal
  • Stale-tasting ice or water from an overdue filter
  • Ovens that bake hotter or cooler than the set temperature
  • Burner flames that have drifted out of adjustment
  • Scale build-up from hard water

How a visit works

  1. You choose the appliances to include. We schedule a visit at a time that suits you.
  2. Your technician works through a checklist for each appliance: cleaning, inspection, adjustment and testing.
  3. You receive a short written report with the condition of each appliance and anything worth watching.
  4. If a repair is needed, we quote it separately. Nothing is done without your approval.

Questions

How often should my appliances be serviced?

For most homes, once a year is right. Kitchens with heavy use, pets or coastal air may benefit from a visit every six months.

What is included in an annual plan?

An annual plan covers a scheduled maintenance visit for the appliances you choose, with a reminder when it is due. Plan details and pricing are confirmed in writing before you enroll.

Can maintenance extend the life of my refrigerator?

A clean condenser and sound gaskets reduce strain on the compressor. That helps the system run as designed and can prevent common failures.

Is maintenance worth it for newer appliances?

Yes. Filters, condensers and seals need care from the first year. Early attention keeps a new appliance performing like one.
