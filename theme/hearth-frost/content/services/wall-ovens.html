Built-in ovens combine precise heating, steam and sophisticated controls. When one of them falters, guesswork does not help. Our technicians understand how these systems work together, and they repair them with genuine parts.

  • Convection ovens
  • Convection steam ovens
  • Speed ovens
  • Microwave drawers and built-in microwaves
  • Warming drawers
  • Vacuum seal drawers
  • Built-in coffee systems

Common problems we fix

  • Oven not heating, or heating unevenly
  • Temperature that drifts from the set point
  • Convection fan noisy or not running
  • Steam oven descaling alerts, water tank or drain faults
  • Door that will not close, latch or lock properly
  • Self-clean cycle that fails or will not unlock
  • Touch controls, displays or error codes
  • Coffee system not brewing, frothing or dispensing

How a visit works

  1. You request a visit with the model and serial number. We confirm an arrival window.
  2. Your technician prepares the work area, then tests heating elements, sensors, fans, steam components and controls.
  3. We explain the findings and give you a written estimate for approval.
  4. We complete the repair, verify performance and clean up before we leave.

Questions

My oven door is locked after a self-clean cycle. What should I do?

Allow the oven to cool fully, which can take more than an hour. If the door still does not release, turn off power at the breaker for one minute and restore it. If the lock remains engaged, schedule a visit.

How often should a steam oven be descaled?

Follow the oven's descaling prompt. In areas with hard water, which includes much of Southern California, the prompt may appear more often. Regular descaling protects the steam generator.

Can you repair a wall oven without removing it from the cabinetry?

Many repairs are done in place. Some require sliding the oven forward. We take care to protect the cabinetry and trim throughout.

Do you service coffee systems?

Yes. We diagnose and repair built-in coffee systems, including brewing, grinding and milk system faults.
