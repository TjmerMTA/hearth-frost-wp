How a visit works

  1. You share the model number and a short description of the symptom. We confirm an arrival window.
  2. Your technician lays protective mats, then checks water supply, drainage, pump, heating and controls.
  3. We give you a written estimate. Work begins only after your approval.
  4. We repair the dishwasher, run a test cycle and check for leaks before we leave.

Questions

Why is there standing water in my dishwasher?

The filter may be blocked, the drain hose kinked or the drain pump faulty. Clean the filter first. If water remains, schedule a visit and avoid running further cycles.

What causes a white film on glassware?

Hard water is the usual cause. An empty rinse aid reservoir or an incorrect water softener setting can make it worse. We can check and adjust these settings during a visit.

Can you adjust my custom door panel?

Yes. We adjust door springs and panel alignment so the door balances correctly and sits flush with the surrounding cabinetry. Cabinetry modifications are outside our scope.

Is it safe to keep using a dishwasher that leaks?

No. A small leak can damage flooring and cabinetry over time. Stop using it, wipe up the water and schedule a visit.
