Questions

Why does my outdoor refrigerator struggle on hot days?

Outdoor units work harder in direct sun and high heat. A dirty condenser, blocked ventilation or worn door gasket can push them past their limit. Cleaning and inspection often restore performance.

How do I protect my grill near the coast?

Salt air accelerates corrosion. Clean stainless surfaces regularly, keep the grill covered when not in use and schedule an annual inspection of igniters and valves.

Do you work on the gas line to the grill?

We service the appliance and its connection. Gas line installation or relocation should be done by a licensed plumber or gas contractor.

Can you service several outdoor appliances in one visit?

Yes. Let us know everything that needs attention when you book, and we will plan enough time.
