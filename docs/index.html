FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Call (800) 555-0142.

Do you handle warranty repairs?

Yes. We service appliances both in and out of the manufacturer warranty and can process eligible warranty claims for you. Have your model and serial number ready when you book.

What does a diagnosis visit cost?

A diagnosis visit has a flat fee that is quoted when you book. If you approve the repair, we tell you the full price before any work begins.

Do you work on older models?

Yes. We service current and legacy models, including units that are twenty years old or more. Parts availability for very old models is confirmed before we schedule.

How soon can a technician come?

In most of our service area we offer appointments within the same week, often sooner for urgent refrigeration problems.

Do you use genuine parts?

Always. We install original manufacturer parts only, which protects performance, safety and any remaining warranty.
